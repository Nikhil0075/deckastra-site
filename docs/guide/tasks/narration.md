# Add narration and choose a voice

1. Open **More modes → Narration & sound**.
2. Add a line on arrival or after a click reveal, or split speaker notes into narration.
3. Choose the speaker selector; the signed-in app loads voices for the recording language.
4. Add pronunciation mappings under **Say names as** when needed.
5. Choose **By click** or **Plays itself** and set the pause between steps.
6. Record a take, use an existing recording, or request voiced lines after reviewing the credit disclosure.
7. Rehearse timing; the deck advances only after both motion and voice finish.
