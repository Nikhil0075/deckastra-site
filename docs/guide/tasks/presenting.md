# Present with ink

1. Choose **Present**.
2. If a second display is available, open the presenter console and place the audience window on the presentation display.
3. Use Right or Space to advance a reveal and Down to move to the next slide.
4. Toggle an ink tool with its numbered shortcut, draw, and use undo/redo for ink.
5. Press C to clear ink on the current slide and B to toggle black screen.
6. Press Escape once to put down an active ink tool; press it again to leave when not full-screen.
