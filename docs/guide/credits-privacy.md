# Credits and privacy

Ordinary editing, deterministic layout, Design Check, and local PDF/PowerPoint export are outside AI credits. Provider-backed generation, translation, and speech use quotes, reservations, and configured limits.

When a provider-backed action is requested, only the content needed for that action is sent to the named provider. Translation sends selected text; speech sends narration text and voice settings; governed generation sends its prompt and required context. Provider credentials remain server-side.

Keep backups of important local decks. Shared copies and exported files are separate from account deletion.

::: warning Documentation, not a legal policy
Public plan/payment rules, legal bases, retention details, operator identity, support contacts, and final deletion commitments require approved legal documents. Draft repository policies are intentionally not published here.
:::
