# Motion that remains part of the document

Object entrances, emphasis, loops, exits, slide transitions, click reveals, and narration timing live in the structured deck rather than in a detached video.

## Author and review motion

- Apply a reviewed motion style across a deck.
- Propose motion by semantic role instead of fragile coordinates.
- Edit object tracks and click boundaries on the timeline.
- Scrub or play the slide before presenting.
- Use **Play motion** while reviewing a pending proposal.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences through explicit fallbacks.

## Present to a second screen

The presenter console shows current and next slides, reveal progress, notes, and elapsed time. Keyboard controls advance reveals before slides, toggle a black screen, open notes, and control ink that appears in the audience window.
