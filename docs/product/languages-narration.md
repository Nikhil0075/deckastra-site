# One deck, multiple language lenses

Language overlays keep translated wording alongside the source while sharing slide geometry, themes, motion, and media. The editor reports missing and outdated translations and lets authors compare with the original without duplicating the deck.

![Live Hindi translation lens](/screenshots/19-multilingual-live-translation.png)

## Narration attached to arrival and click steps

Narration lines can play on slide arrival or after individual reveals. Authors can select from the live voice inventory, add pronunciation mappings, record takes, and choose click-driven or self-playing delivery. A slide advances only after its motion and voice complete.

![Live Hindi voice inventory](/screenshots/20-narration-live-voice-list.png)

Provider-backed translation and speech require sign-in, available credits, and a configured cloud feature. The interface names that boundary rather than presenting cloud work as local.
