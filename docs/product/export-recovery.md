# Export without hiding what changed

Deckastra exports PDF and PowerPoint from the same resolved scene used by the editor. PDF preserves selectable text. PowerPoint favors editable text and native shapes where possible. When a target cannot carry a feature, the degradation ledger records whether it was flattened, approximated, rasterized, or dropped.

![Completed export with degradation report](/screenshots/18-export-degradation-report.png)

## Recovery layers

- Undo and inverse operations for recent work.
- Version history with changed-slide indicators and restore.
- Recovery journal for pending local edits.
- Emergency local copy when the workspace authority is unavailable.
- Backup and restore controls for local workspaces.

![Version history and restore](/screenshots/15-version-history.png)

![Workspace service recovery state](/screenshots/16-recovery-service-down.png)

Export success is not a promise of perfect PowerPoint fidelity. Read the report and inspect representative slides before distribution.
