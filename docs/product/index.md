# Product overview

Deckastra is an AI-native presentation studio for people who want automation without giving up editability or authority. It combines direct authoring, reviewed templates, deterministic composition, connected agents, motion, presentation, multilingual overlays, narration, and export around one structured `.mydeck` document.

## Who it helps

- Founders and product teams shaping an important story quickly.
- Technical teams that need diagrams, code, data, and inspectable structure.
- Educators and speakers who need reveals, narration, language variants, and presenter controls.
- Agent users who want Claude Code, Codex, or another MCP client to contribute without silently taking over the document.

## Three ways to begin

1. Author directly on the canvas.
2. Start from a reviewed template or design language.
3. Ask the built-in assistant or a connected agent to prepare a proposal.

All three routes converge on the same document, transaction, renderer, history, presentation, and export systems.
