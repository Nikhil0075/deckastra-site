# Roadmap

## After the hackathon

- Sign and notarize desktop applications.
- Publish the Windows installer and final supported-system matrix.
- Verify the public web application origin and connect the site CTA.
- Finish hosted-sharing acceptance and operator-approved legal documents.

## Production readiness

- Load-test representative authoring, generation, and export journeys.
- Rehearse recovery with measured objectives and confirm alert delivery.
- Complete security review, abuse controls, and operational runbooks.

## Collaboration and platforms

- Expand collaboration without weakening revision or approval boundaries.
- Test additional desktop architectures and operating-system versions.
- Broaden provider support behind the same quote, credit, and disclosure model.

## Export and presentation

- Improve native PowerPoint mappings where editability and fidelity can coexist.
- Expand presentation controls and audience workflows.
- Add new output forms only when their degradation and timing behavior can be explained honestly.
